Task Reset Companion

A tiny personal helper for ADHD/anxiety task initiation. Not gamified: just practical next steps, self-talk, and calming structure.

How to use: Pick your current state, take one tiny action, and stop after 5 minutes if you want.

8 app concept ideas (non-gamified)

1) State-First Coach
Starts by naming your emotional state, then gives matching strategy scripts.
2) Tiny Step Generator
Turns any vague task into one physical first action.
3) Shame Spiral Interrupt
Catches self-criticism and offers a fast reframe + lower-the-bar reset.
4) Minimum Viable Day Planner
Asks for only 1–3 “today counts if…” items.
5) Re-entry After Drift
Guides getting back on track after a week/month off.
6) Body-Double Board
Simple launch links for co-working/focus sessions.
7) Anxiety-to-Action Triage
Separates urgent-feeling from actually urgent.
8) Evidence Log
Done-list and “proof I can do hard things” log.
